A few answers before you call.

My AC isn’t cooling. How do I reach you?+

Call (561) 780-5220 and tell us what’s happening. We’ll discuss your location, the issue, and current availability. For urgent requests, calling is the best way to reach us.

Can you help with a replacement system?+

Yes. Contact Hawks about AC installation and replacement options for your home or business. We’ll need details about your space and existing system before discussing a suitable solution.

Do you offer maintenance and duct cleaning?+

Yes. Reach out about AC maintenance, duct cleaning, and indoor air quality services. Tell us about your system and any concerns so we can discuss the right service.

Do you work on heating and refrigeration?+

Yes. Hawks provides heating service and installation, plus refrigeration service and repair for residential and commercial customers. Call or email us with your equipment details so we can discuss what you need.

Does an online request book an appointment?+

No. The form sends your service request to Hawks. Your appointment is only scheduled once we confirm availability and details with you directly.

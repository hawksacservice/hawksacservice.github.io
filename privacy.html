WEBSITE PRIVACY

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Service requests

The service request form prepares an email on your device. Entering your details or preparing a request does not send them to Hawks AC Service. You choose whether to open your email app and send the message, or copy it into your webmail. The website does not save your form details in cookies, browser storage, or a website database.

Email and phone contact

When you send an email or call, the information you share is provided to Hawks AC Service so we can respond to your inquiry. Your email provider and telephone provider handle those communications under their own policies. Avoid including payment information or other sensitive information in a service request.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website code does not include advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

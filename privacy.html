WEBSITE PRIVACY

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Service requests

When you submit a service request, your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, and message are sent through FormSubmit to hawksacservice@gmail.com. Hawks uses these details to respond to your request and arrange service. Submitting the form does not book an appointment or add you to a marketing list.

FormSubmit processes submissions for email delivery and spam protection. Its documentation states that submissions are retained for 30 days. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy. Requests received by Hawks remain in our business email unless deleted. To ask about or request deletion of information you have sent us, contact the email below.

The website does not save form details in your browser storage. Please avoid including payment information or other sensitive details in the form.

Email and phone contact

When you send an email or call, the information you share is provided to Hawks AC Service so we can respond to your inquiry. Your email provider and telephone provider handle those communications under their own policies. Avoid including payment information or other sensitive information in a service request.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website code does not include advertising trackers or analytics. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Questions

Contact Hawks AC Service LLC at hawksacservice@gmail.com or (561) 780-5220 with questions about information you have shared with us.
